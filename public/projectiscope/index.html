Journey and sound

Journey folds out to the sphere, becomes a new piece, and folds back in. Sound listens to the mirrors: a bell each time the motif crosses a twisting mirror, a soft pluck for the others, over a drone tuned to the group.

Folded Zodiac

The twelve signs ride a band around the celestial sphere. Fold the sphere into the projective plane and every sign lands on its opposite: Aries on Libra, Taurus on Scorpio. The band itself becomes a Möbius strip.

Aspects

On the sphere, aspects are chords across the chart. After the fold, points 180° apart are the same point, so an aspect of θ and one of 180° − θ become the same relationship: each pair is drawn as an equal-length bow along the band. Tap any line, planet, house number or angle on the chart, or an aspect here, to see what it is.
