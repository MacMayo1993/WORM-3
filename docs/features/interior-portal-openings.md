# WORM interior portal openings

The two outer tile backs now have recessed, open-ended wormhole mouths. Their
positions come from the ridden curve's intersection with the interior wall,
including the lateral displacement of raised corner cubies. The paired backs
use their current antipodal palette colors, with quiet spiral/rib lighting down
the short throat. Their centers contain no disc or end cap.

The mini anticube has cutouts through its stickers **and** plastic body. Every
core material, including equipped tile styles, uses the same world-space bore
along the simulation curve. Cosmetic approach zoom therefore moves the core
around the occupied passage without moving the hole away from the rider. Both
core docks and the central turn are included in the sampled bore. Long outer
shoulders are cropped to the maximum region the enlarged core can occupy.

The openings use the same current/last-tail passage as the exterior portal
effects. They clear when that passage clears. Interior wall, backing, and dimmer
materials share the two wall cutouts; cached exterior tile materials are never
modified. The interior group is hidden outside the camera's interior phase,
including its depth-writing backing, so no invisible wall remains in front of
the crawling worm. Pause and reduced motion freeze the mouth lighting.

There are two additional mouth draws during the interior view, with no offscreen
views or stencil passes. Core holes add fragment clipping to existing draws.
Travel, collision, ribbon geometry, and camera paths are unchanged.
